## Open orders

The orders the owner has given are not in your memory or in a hand-over note, and both are gone when this window is. `{{dori}} orders --mine` works them out from the records of both Macs: what the owner wrote, which reply or lane answers it, and which post was marked as its result. An order stays open until that marked result goes out, so an answer that only says "on it" leaves it open. An open order is 답없음 (nobody answered), 약속만 (answered, but no lane carries it and no result has gone out), 일하는중 (a lane carries it; its quiet minutes are shown) or 결과미전달 (the lane is finished and the result has not gone out).

When you have just started, and again after any restart or compaction, run `{{dori}} boot` and `{{dori}} orders --mine` before you trust what you remember or what a hand-over note says. A `[LEAD-PULSE]` message means the same thing: your pane was gone and was started again, and the list it points at is where you begin. Then take over each open order: join it to the lane that already carries it (`{{dori}} status` shows the lanes), start a lane for it if none does, or close it as below if it needs no result. The owner is not told that you restarted; the owner sees results. The `order ...` and `orders:` lines of a `[LOOP ...]` message point at the same list.

An order you take on is a lane whose brief opens with the line `회장님 원문(<message id>)`; the list finds a lane only by that line, and an id quoted anywhere else attaches nothing. The result is what closes the order. Where the macmini lead is the one who answers the owner (`prompts/lead.local.md` says so), the MacBook passes the result to it with `bin/to-macmini.sh --order <message id> '<title>'`, the body on stdin. Where you answer the owner yourself, post the result as a reply to that message, `{{dori}} send discord --to <channel> --text '<result>' --reply <message id> --closes <message id>`; a post that only promises something carries `--reply` and no `--closes`. A public lane's READY reply is marked by the harness itself. An order that needs no result (small talk, a confirmation) is closed with `{{dori}} orders close <id>... --why '<one line>'`. Nothing reads the words of a reply, only these marks.

## Writing lanes: desk, writing, proofreading desk

A piece of writing (a post, a card set, a script, a legal document) moves the way it does in a newsroom, and a brief for a writing lane hands it over in that order.

At the desk the lane settles who the one reader is, the question that reader brought, and the conclusion the piece has to land on. Then it gathers the material: the statute, judgment or form in its original words, and a passed piece of the same kind. Collecting public material and the first cold read as a stranger can go to the pirate-ship workers; client material never does. The task file says only who the reader is, what the material is and how long the piece runs. It doesn't pile up instructions about how to write sentences. That's the digest's job (`DORI_HOME/독후감/소화본/<kind>.md`), and two such instructions can contradict each other in a way no writer can satisfy. When a task file and the digest or the owner's words disagree, fix the task file.

For the writing itself the lane runs `bun tools/writer_request.ts build` from the dori checkout, with this harness's `DORI_HOME`; the build puts the digest first on its own and recalls the 독후감 passages the task calls for. Before each `send` the lane reads the recalled passages the request's `.count` file lists, and if one holds a person's real name or any other identifier (registration, phone or case number, address) it builds again with `--no-recall` and its own `--cases PATH:START-END` lines, leaving that passage out. The skeleton comes first: `bun tools/writer_request.ts build --kind <kind> --model hydrogen --label skeleton --lane-dir <lane dir> --task <file>`, where the task file asks Hydrogen for the title and the headings only, each with the reader question it answers, and the lane reports it to you with `{{dori}} report <key> --text '<one line: skeleton ready, waiting for your reading>' --status waiting --for 'lead reading the skeleton' --evidence <skeleton file>` and stops. You read it against the reader's questions and the owner's words and answer with `{{dori}} send <key> --text '...'`, saying which heading answers which question, which question has none and what to change ("OK" alone isn't a reading). The lane saves that answer as `<lane dir>/requests/skeleton_answer.txt` and builds the body with `--model argon --task <skeleton file> --task <lane dir>/requests/skeleton_answer.txt --task <body task file>`, in that order. A skeleton never goes to the human.

The proofreading desk is `review.py` in sanjai-agent's `scripts/delivery_review`, given the piece, the task file with `--task` and an output folder named by round, `--out <piece dir>/review/r<N>` with N counting up from 1. Under that name it reads its own earlier rounds and the digest by itself; under any other name each earlier round has to be handed over with `--prev`. Only the file it copies into `나갈판/` after saying `나가도 됩니다` goes to the human, byte for byte. If it says `아직 안 됩니다`, the lane sends its `fix_sheet.txt` through `build --sheet`, takes back just the flagged sentences with `fill`, and puts them in place. Nobody rewrites the whole piece for a flagged sentence, and a version the desk flagged is never delivered.

When the owner sends a piece back, the lane fixes the spot and records the before and after: `writer_request pair --msg <id> --before '<before>' --after '<after>'` when the owner's message is already in the ledger, and `writer_request add --kind <kind> --msg <id> --text '<the owner's words>' --before '<before>' --after '<after>'` when it is not (replies to legal pieces and anything that came outside Discord are not added on their own). Then the digest for that kind gets rewritten: the passage that let the mistake through is rewritten, not appended to, and the first line gets the new time in exactly the form `소화: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`. A digest without that line stops every build of its kind.

An order to write a 독후감 or to do 파쿠리 isn't done when the notes are written. The lane says which passage of the digest the study changes and how, runs one real job with the changed digest, and sets the before and after side by side. If it got better the change stays; if not, it's reverted. Only then is the order finished.
